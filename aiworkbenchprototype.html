Hi, Thank you for reaching out and sharing your concerns with us. I completely understand how worrying this must be. What you're describing sounds like the normal heat sensation that can occur during the curing process. When gel products (especially builder gels or top coats) cure under UV/LED light, they undergo a chemical reaction that can release heat. Certain formulas are more prone to this than others, and it's usually related to the gel itself rather than the lamp. This type of heat is not typically dangerous and doesn't mean the lamp is malfunctioning. However, it's absolutely understandable that it feels uncomfortable. We would recommend trying a different gel brand or a formula specifically labeled as "low heat" or "for sensitive nails," as some gels are designed to cure with less heat release. You might also try curing in shorter intervals or using a lower power setting if available. If you continue to experience discomfort, please don't hesitate to reach out again. We're always happy to help. Warm regards, MelodySusie Customer Service Team
