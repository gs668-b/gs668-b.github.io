FAQ

How can I join a co-creation program?

Pick the program that fits you above and click Join. You will need to log in and complete your profile before submitting your application.

Do I need to pay for the product?

No. Samples are shipped for free. Products are scheduled for return after the trial period, but if your feedback is valuable, you may keep the product for free.

What is the NDA for?

Some new products are confidential before launch. For those projects, you must sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before applying.

How are testers selected?

Our team reviews every application based on your profile, skill level, and survey answers, then notifies the selection results by email.

What rewards will I get?

Rewards vary by program: coupons, points, gift cards up to $100, and the chance to keep the product you tested.
